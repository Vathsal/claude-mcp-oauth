claude-mcp

A personal automation tool. Not a public product.

claude-mcp connects one person's own Google account (Gmail, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Calendar, Slides) to Claude, an AI assistant, running on that person's own computer. It lets the assistant read and organize that person's email and documents on request.

It has one user: its owner. There is no sign-up and no service offered to anyone else.
