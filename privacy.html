Privacy policy

claude-mcp · last updated October 29, 2026

Who uses it

claude-mcp is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent, it accesses Gmail, Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, Calendar, Slides and Forms data in the owner's account.

How data is used

Data is used only to carry out the owner's own requests, such as searching email or editing a document. It is not sold, shared with third parties for advertising, or used to build profiles.

Where data is stored

OAuth tokens are stored on the owner's own computer. No separate server stores Google data. Content the owner asks about is sent to the AI assistant the owner uses to fulfill that request.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Use the support email shown on the Google consent screen.
